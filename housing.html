Every student has different needs and preferences. Explore the types of campus housing students may choose from at OBU.

  • Traditional Residence Halls: Shared rooms in a residence hall.
  • Suite-Style Living: Bedrooms with shared common areas.
  • Apartment-Style Housing: A more independent living arrangement.

What Is Dorm Mate?

Dorm Mate is OBU's roommate-matching platform, designed to help students find compatible roommates before moving into campus housing.

Students can create a profile and share their interests, lifestyle, preferences, and daily routines. These details can help students discover potential roommates with similar habits and expectations.
